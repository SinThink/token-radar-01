Method

Value model

For each protocol, implied_value_usd = 10^(3.4745 + 0.4513 × log10(TVL)) × (1 + 0.25 × g). g is the 7-day TVL change as a fraction, clamped to [-0.60, +1.20]; missing change is 0. The line comes from a Huber robust regression of log10(market cap) on log10(TVL) across 99 comparables (protocols with a positive market cap and TVL, excluding CEX, versioned names, stable categories, and canonical/cross-chain bridges). The growth tilt is fixed, not fitted. Age, market-cap floors and category effects were tested against time-split validation and dropped — they made the fit worse.

Validation

  • Leave-one-out median miss ×2.83; within 3× 50.5%; within 10× 75.8%.
  • Time-split (fit on protocols listed before 2024-01-01, test on 2024+): ×2.16.
  • Newest-33 time-split (fit on older comparables, test on the 33 newest): ×2.76.
  • Flat multiple (market cap / TVL) baseline: leave-one-out ×5.05, within 3× 35.4%.

Farm economics

Ticket = $150–$350, smaller for younger and smaller protocols, larger for the big names. Airdrop share = user-selected, default 10% of the implied value scaled to the ticket against a $5M reference float. Hold = 90–180 days depending on protocol size and age. Cash fees = round-trip protocol fee per chain. Gas = documented per-chain budget. Forgone yield = 4% annual T-bill rate over the hold period. Hands-on hours = setup + work, scaled with chain count. Net per hour = net / hands-on hours. Verdict: ROBUST if it pays at band P10, FARM if it pays at point value, THIN if it beats cash fees only, SKIP if fees eat the drop.

Caveats

"Records no token" is DefiLlama's record, not proof that no token exists. The implied value is the entry's own claim, not a forecast of a launch, not make.fun's. Costs are assumptions; real fees and gas differ by position size, congestion and chain state.
