These protocols' records carry a token with a market cap, so their market cap is a fact, not a model.       Honest caveats

      • "Records no token" is DefiLlama's record, never proof that no token exists: a protocol here may still carry a token its record does not show.
      • The implied and expected values are this entry's own claim, not a forecast of a launch and not make.fun's.
      • Gas, fees, hold and the airdrop share are stated assumptions. Real fees, congestion and pool state differ, and the minimum viable capital moves with them.
      • The odds term is fitted on one frozen snapshot: it is a calibrated propensity over that census, not a time-series forecast of a launch.
